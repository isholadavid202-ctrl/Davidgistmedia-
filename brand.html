Using the logo

  • Keep it on one line. Never stack DAVID, GIST and MEDIA.
  • Leave space. Clear space on every side equals the height of the icon.
  • Keep the colours. Only black, white and signal red. GIST is always the red word.
  • Do not stretch, tilt or add shadows to the logo.
