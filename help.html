Help & FAQs

How do I get new stories in my inbox?
Use the "Never Miss a Story" box on the homepage. Enter your email and tap Subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I search for a story?
Use the search icon at the top of the page and type what you are looking for.

Can I comment on stories?
Yes. Open a story and use the comments section under it. We remove comments that are abusive or break the law.

I found a mistake in a story.
Tell us on the Corrections page.

How can I send a story tip or contact the team?
Use the contact page.

Where can I follow Davidgistmedia?
On TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. The links are at the bottom of every page.
