Help & FAQs

Quick answers about your account, comments, the app and more.

How do I create an account or sign in?

Tap Sign in at the top right of any page and follow the steps. An account lets you comment on stories.

Why can’t I comment on a story?

You need to be signed in to comment. After you sign in, go back to the story and write your comment. Comments that break our rules may be removed.

How do I like or share a story?

At the bottom of each story you will find the Like and Share buttons. There are also share icons for Facebook, X, email, Telegram and WhatsApp near the top of the story.

How do I get the Davidgistmedia app?

On Android, tap Download on the app banner that appears on the site. On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen.

How do I switch to dark mode?

Tap the moon icon at the top right of any page. Tap it again to go back to light mode. The site remembers your choice.

How do I get stories by email?

Use the Never miss a story box at the bottom of any page, type your email address and tap Subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.

I found a mistake in a story. What should I do?

Please tell us through the contact page, with the link to the story. Read how we handle corrections.

How do I follow Davidgistmedia?

Join our WhatsApp channel or Telegram channel, or follow us on X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

How is my information used?

Read our Privacy Policy and our Cookies page.
