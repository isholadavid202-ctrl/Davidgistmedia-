Privacy Policy

How Davidgistmedia collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information Davidgistmedia collects when you use our website and app, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that apply to us.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in or create an account, we receive details such as your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Comments: what you write and the name you post under.
  • Newsletter: your email address if you subscribe to our emails.
  • Usage information: pages you visit, the device and browser you use, and your approximate location, collected with analytics tools.
  • Information on your device: small items saved in your browser, such as your light or dark mode choice, stories you liked and a copy of recent stories to help pages load faster.

How we use it

  • To run the Service, show you stories and let you comment and sign in.
  • To send the emails you asked for.
  • To understand how the Service is used and make it better.
  • To show advertising and measure how it performs.
  • To keep the Service safe and prevent abuse.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense and other advertising services to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more at aboutads.info. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and understand which stories readers enjoy. It collects information such as pages viewed and device details.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies. Read more on our Cookies page, including how to control them.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Service (such as hosting, database, analytics and advertising providers), when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and our readers.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires, and then delete or anonymise it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. You can also clear the information saved in your browser. To make a request, contact us.

Children

The Service is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website can promise complete security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
