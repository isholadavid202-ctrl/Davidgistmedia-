Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains what information Davidgistmedia ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, and how it is used.

Information we collect

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the email address you provide. If you post a comment, we collect the name (or "Anonymous") and comment text you submit. We do not require you to create an account to read articles or leave comments.

How we use it

Email addresses collected through the newsletter form are used only to send you updates about new stories. Comments are displayed publicly beneath the article they were posted on.

Cookies and similar technology

This site may use cookies or browser local storage for basic functionality, such as remembering which articles you've liked so you can't like the same story twice. We do not use tracking cookies to build advertising profiles. If we add analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) or advertising (such as Google AdSense) in the future, those services may set their own cookies, and this policy will be updated to reflect that.

Third parties

We use Supabase to store site data and Render to host the site. These providers may process data on our behalf as part of running the service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by contacting us. You can request that we delete a comment you posted by emailing us with a link to it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to davidgistmediatv@gmail.com.
