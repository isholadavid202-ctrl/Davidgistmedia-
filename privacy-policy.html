Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Davidgistmedia ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit davidgistmedia.onrender.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. If you post a comment, we collect the name you enter and the text of your comment. If you contact us by email or phone, we keep the details you send us so that we can reply.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our partners may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, the time and date of your visit, and the website you came from. We also record actions such as liking a story.

How we use information

We use information to run and improve the Site, to show you stories, to send newsletters to people who subscribed, to display and moderate comments, to understand how the Site is used, to keep it secure, and to show advertising.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, measure traffic, and show and measure ads. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Site may not work properly if you block them.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and how long visitors stay. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads on the Site. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising.

Data storage and service providers

Comments, likes, newsletter subscriptions and our stories are stored with our database provider, Supabase. We also rely on our hosting provider, Render, to deliver the Site. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy terms.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers described above, when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our visitors and the Site.

Comments are public

Comments you post appear publicly on the Site together with the name you enter. Please do not include personal information you do not want others to see. You can ask us to remove your comment by email.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we use your email address only to send you our stories and updates. You can ask us to remove your email address at any time by writing to us.

External links and embedded content

The Site may link to or embed content from other websites, such as YouTube. These sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain uses of it. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the Site means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent to davidgistmediatv@gmail.com.
