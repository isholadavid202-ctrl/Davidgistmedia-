About Davidgistmedia

Davidgistmedia is an independent Nigerian news and media platform covering entertainment, sports, business, and current affairs. We aim to deliver timely, clearly presented stories to readers across Nigeria and beyond.

The newsroom is run by Ishola David, along with contributors who help bring stories to the site. We are committed to accuracy and to correcting mistakes quickly when they happen — see our Editorial & Corrections Policy for details on how we handle that.

For questions, tips, or feedback, visit our Contact page or reach us directly at davidgistmediatv@gmail.com.
