About Davidgistmedia

Davidgistmedia is a news and entertainment platform covering Nigeria and the world. We publish stories on entertainment, sports, business, politics, technology and breaking news from home and abroad, with a focus on keeping readers informed quickly and accurately.

What we cover

  • Nigeria and local newsStories that matter to readers at home.
  • EntertainmentMusic, film, celebrities and culture.
  • Sports, business and technologyResults, markets, and the ideas shaping the future.
  • World and politicsMajor events and decisions, explained clearly.
  • Podcasts and live coverageConversations and live updates, on the site and on our social channels.

Who runs it

Davidgistmedia was founded by Ishola David, who is the Founder and CEO and writes and publishes stories on the site.

Our standards

We aim to be accurate and fair. Our Editorial Policy explains how we choose, check and publish stories. If we get something wrong, we correct it openly. See our Corrections page to report an error.
