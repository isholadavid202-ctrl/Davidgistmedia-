About us

Davidgistmedia is a news and entertainment platform covering Nigeria and the world.

Who we are

Davidgistmedia brings readers breaking news, entertainment, sport, business, politics, technology and more from Nigeria, Africa and around the world. We publish quickly, and we work to keep what we publish accurate and clearly sourced.

What we cover

  • Nigeria, Africa and local news
  • Politics and business
  • Entertainment and fashion
  • Sport, including football, basketball, athletics and boxing
  • Technology, science and education
  • Europe and world news

Where to find us

You can read Davidgistmedia on this website and on your phone, where you can install our app from the banner on the site. We also publish videos, podcasts and live coverage, and share stories on our WhatsApp and Telegram channels.

Our founder

Davidgistmedia was founded by Ishola David, who is also its CEO. He writes and publishes stories on the site, with a focus on keeping readers informed quickly and accurately.

Our standards

We hold ourselves to clear rules on accuracy, sourcing, corrections and the way we separate news from opinion and advertising. You can read them in our Editorial Policy.
