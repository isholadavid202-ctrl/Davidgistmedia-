Corrections

We aim to be accurate. When we get something wrong, we correct it quickly and openly.

How we handle corrections. If a story contains a factual error, we fix it and add a note at the bottom of the story saying what changed. We do not quietly change the facts in a published story.

Report an error. Send us a message on the contact page. Please include the headline or link of the story, what is wrong and, if you can, a source that shows the correct information.

Our full standards are in our Editorial Policy.
