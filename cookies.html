Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Davidgistmedia uses them to keep the site working, understand how it is used and show advertising.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to count visitors and see which stories are read. This data is combined and does not name you.

Advertising. We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings.

Site preferences. We may store small settings on your device, such as your theme choice.

Controlling cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work properly if you do.

For more on how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy. Questions? Contact us.
