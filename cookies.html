Cookies

What cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files saved on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, which work in the same way. They help a website work, remember your choices and understand how it is used.

How we use them

  • Essential and preference: keeping you signed in, remembering your light or dark mode choice, remembering stories you liked and keeping a copy of recent stories so pages load faster. We also remember when you close the app banner.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics helps us count visits and see which stories are read.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense and other partners use cookies to show and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits.

Controlling cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block them, parts of the site, such as signing in, may not work properly.

To manage personalised advertising, visit Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
