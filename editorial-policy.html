Editorial Policy

The standards we hold ourselves to when we report and publish.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Accuracy

We aim to be accurate and fair. We check facts before we publish, and we say clearly when something is not yet confirmed.

Sources and credit

We name our sources whenever we can. When we use information, pictures or video from another organisation or person, we credit them. We do not pass off other people’s work as our own.

Independence

Our reporting is independent. Advertisers, partners and supporters do not decide what we publish. We tell readers when there is a connection that matters.

News, analysis and opinion

We keep news reporting separate from analysis and opinion. Analysis pieces are marked as Analysis, so readers know what they are reading.

Advertising and sponsored content

Advertising and sponsored content are clearly marked and are kept separate from our news. See Advertise with us.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we want to put it right quickly. We correct errors of fact, and when a story changes in a way that matters we say so, for example with an “Updated” date on the story.

To report a mistake, please contact us with the link to the story and what you think is wrong.

Comments

Readers need an account to comment. We may remove comments that are abusive, unlawful, misleading or off-topic. See our Terms of Use.

External links

We link to other websites when we think it helps readers, for example to show a source. A link is not an endorsement, and we are not responsible for the content of external sites.
