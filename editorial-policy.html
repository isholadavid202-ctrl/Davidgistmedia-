Editorial & Corrections Policy

Our standards

Davidgistmedia aims to report news accurately and fairly. We try to verify information before publishing and to clearly separate factual reporting from opinion where relevant.

Sourcing

Where a story is based on another outlet's reporting, we aim to credit that source. Where a story is original reporting, it reflects our own research.

Mistakes happen

If we get something wrong, we will correct it. Significant corrections will be noted at the bottom of the affected article along with the date of the correction.

Reporting an error

If you believe an article contains an inaccuracy, please email davidgistmediatv@gmail.com with a link to the story and a description of the issue. We review every report we receive.
