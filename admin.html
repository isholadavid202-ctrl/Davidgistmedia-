Live Audio

Shows on the Live Audio page (live-audio.html), separate from the video Live page. Paste one of these when you go live: audio: followed by your stream link (for example audio:https://your-stream-address/live), a YouTube live link, or the embed code from your streaming service. Tap "Stop live audio" when you're done.

Videos (short and long)

Posts to the Videos page (videos.html). Short videos play in a swipe feed, long videos in a grid. For long videos, uploading to YouTube and pasting the link works best.
