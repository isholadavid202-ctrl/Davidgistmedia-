Advertise with us

Reach readers across Nigeria and beyond with Davidgistmedia.

Why advertise with us

Davidgistmedia is read by people who follow news, entertainment, sport and business in Nigeria and around the world. We offer clear, well-placed advertising that respects our readers.

Ways to work with us

  • Display advertising: banner and page placements across the site.
  • Sponsored content: stories and features written with or for a brand. Sponsored content is always labelled as such.
  • Partnerships: events, campaigns and coverage built around your launch or announcement.
  • Social channels: promotion on our WhatsApp, Telegram and social media accounts.

Our rules

  • Advertising is kept separate from our editorial decisions. Advertisers do not decide what we report.
  • Advertising and sponsored content are clearly marked, so readers can tell them from news.
  • We do not accept advertising that is misleading, illegal or harmful.
