Accessibility Help

We want everyone to be able to read Davidgistmedia.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Our commitment

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use Davidgistmedia. We work towards the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, and keep improving the site.

What helps

  • Dark mode: tap the moon icon at the top of any page for a darker, easier-on-the-eyes view.
  • Bigger text: use your browser or phone settings to make text larger. Our pages adjust to fit.
  • Clear headings and menus: pages use headings and a simple menu to make them easier to move through, including with a keyboard or a screen reader.
  • Sharing and reading: you can listen to our podcasts and watch our videos as well as read our stories.

Known limits

Some older stories, pictures and videos may not yet have full text descriptions, and some third-party content such as advertising and embedded video may not be fully accessible. We are working to improve this.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something hard to use, or need information in a different format, please contact us. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will do our best to help.
