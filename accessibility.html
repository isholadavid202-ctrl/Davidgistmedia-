Accessibility Help

We want everyone to be able to read Davidgistmedia, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Make text bigger. On most phones, open Settings, then Display, then Font size. In your browser you can also pinch the screen to zoom in.

Dark mode. Use your phone's dark theme or your browser's dark mode setting if you find a white background tiring to read.

Screen readers. Our pages use headings, labelled buttons and link text so that screen readers such as TalkBack and VoiceOver can move through them.

Keyboard use. You can move through links and buttons with the Tab key.

If you find something on the site hard to use, tell us on the contact page. Say which page it was and what device you were using, and we will work to fix it.
